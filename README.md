# 📊 Sales Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Sales Analytics Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard analyzes six months of sales data (January–June) to monitor business performance and identify meaningful sales insights.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Identify top-performing products.
- Evaluate monthly and daily sales trends.
- Compare salesperson performance.
- Understand product category distribution.

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Sales | 562K |
| Average Sales | 7K |
| Total Orders | 80 |
| Total Discount | 21K |

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

- March recorded the highest monthly sales at 125K.
- February recorded the lowest monthly sales at 42K.
- Cooking Oil was the top product by sales value at 105K.
- Karim Mia recorded the highest salesperson-wise sales at approximately 142K.
- Pen recorded the highest quantity sold at 159 units.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

1. Monthly Sales Analysis
2. Category-wise Sales Distribution
3. Product-wise Quantity Analysis
4. Daily Sales Trends
5. Top 5 Product Performance
6. Salesperson-wise Performance
